LPI Development 2017

This was one of my first branding projects for a real client to which I look back with much fondness. I'm very hapy that LPI Development trusted me with going into an abstract option for their brand mark.

THE BRIEF was to create a brand for a small scale development company with an idea of an all-inclusive service that only starts with selling appartments. LPI Development’s business aim is to have a personal contact with client and cater to their every need – moving home, financing, interior design and smart home, etc.

Icons that represent different bonus services offered by LPI Development: financing, interior design, smart home, interior finishing, moving home, promotions
