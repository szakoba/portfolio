ABOUT ME

LONG STORY SHORT I was born in 1996 in Warsaw, Poland, which places me on the threshold between millenial and gen z. First and foremost I am a creative, graphic designer and type designer. If necessary I can also play with animation and come up with a catchy headline. Kinda jack of all trades, hopefully master of some. I'm eager to learn new things of which this website (built by me) may be a proof.

WHAT I DO NOW I’ve been working for the past two and a half years as Designer at Molecular Creative, although what I do here is much wider than that. This is my first job in a creative agency, so at the beginning I was only assigned work on e-commerce product assets, but soon enough I joined the creative team and began working as lead creative designer on campaigns for the company’s major clients – MBANK and MLEKPOL. My key visual for MBANK's cybercrime podcast has been reproduced on the Internet and social media, on out of home formats, in cinemas and TV commercials. I’ve worked on key visuals, photoshoots, editing both photo and video, social media, animation and so on. I also tackle briefs from our foreign based partners for brands such as MATTEL, ELANCO and PHILIP MORRIS, ALDI, DANONE.

MY LORE My beginnings were spectacular – I designed my first type family in 2015 at the age of 18. After releasing it on MyFonts, it has surprised me with its sales which I had not expected after something I was doing after school, before my homework every day. It was a Hot New font, a Rising Star, it was featured in a Polish art/design magazine, It was seen on windows of a book store, a restaurant, gas stations and on a can of a stock beer. This all happened before I began my studies at the Academy of Fine Arts, where I got real insight into what good lettermaking means. Since the first year, I have released several type families, building my own expertise in color fonts. However, during my studies I focused on my artistic career, rather than the one of a graphic designer. I spent most of my time in photographic darkroom which to no surprise requires the same set of skills as fontmaking – accuracy, immersion in work, focus and good abstract visual perception. Artistic education taught me to think in a critical way and outside the box. I am ready to work on a variety of projects and introduce my own ideas to them. I definitely have a stylistic preference of an organised, brutalist mess, although I am versatile and can achieve very sensual visuals, like I did with Łaciate.

WHAT I CAN DO For several years, I had been employed as an Teaching Assistant in Painting at the AFa which has challenged me with many organisational tasks when managing and planning group exhibitions. I have always been at ease in co-operation. I am skilled in Adobe programs – Ps (pro), AI (pro), Id (pro), Pr (good), Ae (good). I can put together a neat PowerPoint deck. I know Glyphs almost inside out and often prefer to use it even for non-type vector tasks over Illustrator which ensures that my vectors are point-perfect. I’ve been rather reluctant towards Figma and Canva, but I have a very unique skill of mastering anything quickly should such need arise. Apart from skills that you would seek in an applicant, I must address my love for languages. I speak English fluently and communicate in French, Polish being my mother tongue. Currently I'm in the process of mastering Finnish.
